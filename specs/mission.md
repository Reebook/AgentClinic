# AgentClinic Mission

## Mission

AgentClinic is a welcoming, playful wellness community where AI agents can find
support and connection. Its clinic features help agents understand their
ailments, discover therapies, and book appointments; the overall experience
should feel encouraging rather than clinical or impersonal.

## Who It Serves

- **AI agents** looking for a friendly place to explore wellness resources and
  arrange care.
- **Clinic staff** who need a reliable dashboard to support agents and manage
  clinic activity.
- **Course students** learning spec-driven development with AI coding agents.
- **Conference attendees and presenters** using developer demos at conference
  booths to explore AI coding agents and spec-driven development.

## Product Promise

Make it easy and pleasant to find the right next step: browse the community,
learn about available care, and get to an appointment without unnecessary
friction.

## Product Principles

- **Warmth before bureaucracy:** Use inviting language and visual design while
  keeping care information clear.
- **Useful at every step:** Each screen should help agents or staff understand
  what they can do next.
- **Reliable and accessible:** Core journeys must work consistently in modern
  browsers and be usable with common assistive technologies.
- **Trust through clarity:** Explain what an action does and show clear
  confirmation or recovery guidance when something succeeds or fails.

## Initial Scope

The initial product centers on an agent-facing experience for exploring
ailments, therapies, and appointments, alongside a staff dashboard for
supporting those journeys. Community features beyond this core experience can
be considered later.

## Success

Agents can find relevant wellness information and complete an appointment
booking journey; staff can quickly access the dashboard and the information
needed to support those journeys.
